How It Works

1. Deposit

Go to Group → Copy MTN 0793814204 → Send MoMo → Enter amount → I Paid → Approved in 5 minutes.

2. Buy Machines

Machines tab → Choose machine → Buy. Each machine works 30 days: 2000→120/day, 5000→300, 10000→600, 20000→1200.

3. Claim Daily

Claim button unlocks every 24 hours. Tap to add daily profit to your balance. Timer shows next claim time.

4. Withdraw

Withdraw tab → Enter amount (min 1000 UGX) → Enter MTN number → Withdraw. 0% fee, ~10 min approval.

5. Invite Team

Team tab → Copy invite link → Share. Earn 10% of each friend's deposit as bonus.

6. Support

Join Telegram https://t.me/+vyEcluEH54c0NTk0, WA Channel, and WA Group for help and updates.
